## Current trusted boundary

Meta POST authentication verifies HMAC over original raw bytes before reading receiving metadata. Both handlers reject a supplied foreign receiving-number ID through `resolveWhatsAppCredentials()`, which binds to the configured single-business canonical number and shared token. A valid Meta signature establishes authenticity to the app secret; it does not establish that the number belongs to RepliQ sales or that a selected record belongs to that number.

The demo selector additionally requires exact `REPLIQ_US_DEMO_SALES_ENABLED=true`, a server-only `REPLIQ_US_DEMO_SALES_BINDING` with a matching numeric receiving ID, fixed purpose/version and nonempty ownership/isolation review references. Browser fields, country, language, form offer and unsigned metadata cannot select it. The signed ID cannot be replaced by a configured fallback. Those references are attestations, not self-verifying account ownership.

The binding still accepts only `storageMode: isolated-redis-only`. Any nonempty `SUPABASE_URL` or `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` rejects requested demo processing. Supplying a new storage label, changing a review reference, claiming a customer owner or copying the canonical ID does not enable Supabase. Do not unset Supabase variables to bypass this restriction: that would violate the required existing storage/dashboard behavior.

When inactive, both stores and the legacy receipt behavior continue exactly as before: `/api/webhook` mirrors receipts into Supabase and Redis when configured; `/api/conversation` updates Redis on its current status branch. That existing difference was tested and preserved, not silently redesigned. Custom business prompts, Moroccan prices, credential selection, AI providers, voice, fail-safe takeover and dashboard contracts remain unchanged.

## Minimum safe future path while retaining Supabase + Redis

There are two materially different cases. Neither is established by this task.

1. **Exclusive single-business deployment and stores:** independently establish that the canonical receiving number is RepliQ-owned sales/demo, that all relevant records and identities in **both** stores belong solely to that business, and that every writer/admin/notification recipient is within that same boundary. Preserve existing UUIDs/messages and canonical keys; do not migrate/delete history just to change a prompt. A separately reviewed deployment-level store-ownership binding could then permit the existing single-business Supabase/Redis stack without pretending it serves multiple tenants. It must include a private reviewed inventory of all conversations/messages/aliases/receipt mappings, provenance of ownership, approval of existing offers/configs, exclusive writer access, account/store resource identities and a documented rollback. A claimed “approved” string, Redis owner key or a single matched phone ID is insufficient. Existing Moroccan contracts must still retain their approved handling. If records or offers are mixed/unknown, this option is blocked.
2. **Two or more businesses sharing infrastructure:** complete the coordinated Phase 1B design first. Immutable business and number ownership, server-verified membership/roles, composite customer/message uniqueness, ownership-constrained message/conversation relationships, explicit business+number filters on every service-role operation, scoped Redis history/identity/owner/takeover/dedup/receipt/config/template/order/index operations, authorized dashboard/manual sends, and recipient/credential scoping must move together. Preserve existing conversation UUIDs, message relations and known business configs. Quarantine unknown ownership rather than guess it; never use another tenant's legacy history as a fallback. Phase 1B SQL stays review-only and its runtime helpers stay inactive.

The second case needs a database/application/authentication rollout and cannot be proven under the present no-migration/no-production-access scope. The first requires external ownership evidence not present locally and a separate approved implementation/review to lift the Supabase restriction. **Neither alternative is activated or exposed as a new storage-mode flag by this branch.** Future client traffic must use separately approved isolated resources until the shared-store design is complete; do not provision or reroute anything as part of this task.

Until then the safe operational path is the existing human-led demo/request follow-up, with existing business prompts and conversations intact. No new US prompt, quota enforcement or billing action is activated.
